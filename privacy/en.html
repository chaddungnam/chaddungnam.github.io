    3. Purposes and Legal Bases

    • Providing the games, accounts, account linking and loading, rankings, Friend features, mailbox, invitations, purchases, restoration, in-app reports and support: contract performance or pre-contractual steps (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
    • Payment, refund, fraud checks, and legally required records: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
    • Security, fair play, stability, and error investigation necessary to operate and protect the service: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). Essential account, currency, transaction, and security processing is separate from optional behavioral analytics.
    • House Duck first-party usage analytics stored in Supabase (including the stability diagnostics above), Firebase Analytics, and the use of the tutorial-completion event for ad-campaign measurement: separate optional consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), withdrawable at any time.
    • Personalized advertising and processing that requires tracking consent: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which may be withdrawn at any time.
    • Limited/non-personalized advertising and fraud prevention: consent or legitimate interests where permitted by applicable law.

    The legitimate interests are protecting accounts and transactions, preventing fraudulent scores or duplicate rewards, investigating errors, handling reports efficiently, and maintaining service stability. Random installation/session identifiers do not by themselves make data anonymous. Behavioral analysis such as screen navigation or button interactions is not treated as essential security processing, and accepting the terms alone does not satisfy any required consent.

    6. Retention

    • Detailed play, season and weekly records and account-separated first-party analytics events: normally 28 days from the record-specific time explained below.
    • Account, current synchronized state, best records, progress and fair-play/reward records: until account deletion.
    • In-app reports: while needed to handle the report, and no longer than until account deletion.
    • Mailbox items: deleted 90 days after they are claimed or expire.
    • Entitlements, transaction references, refund and fraud records: as needed to support the purchase or for the applicable statutory retention period.
    • Support emails: as needed to resolve the request and handle disputes.
    • Local settings: until the app or device data is deleted.
    • Information independently held by store, sign-in, advertising, analytics, and hosting providers: under each provider's retention policy and settings.

    How retention is calculated: daily cleanup removes data older than 28 days based on the play time for detailed gameplay, the update/achievement time for season/weekly records, and server receipt time for first-party analytics. The next daily run can normally add up to one day after that threshold. The 28-day window does not apply uniformly to Firebase analytics, push tokens, or providers’ independent logs.

    The app-managed unsent first-party analytics queue and pending Firebase custom-event queue expire 24 hours after event creation. Expired entries are discarded at the next queue check or before transmission; both queues are also discarded upon withdrawal or account switching. This does not mean deletion occurs at exactly 24 hours while the app is closed. It does not set a blanket limit for Firebase SDK internal storage or providers’ independent retention, and does not guarantee immediate deletion of information already transmitted.

    7. Account Deletion

    You can delete your game account in the app under Settings → Delete Account or follow the external deletion instructions. The account profile, synchronized state, progress, friends and social connections, mailbox, invitations, in-app reports and fair-play records linked to the account are deleted and cannot be recovered; a Sign in with Apple link is also revoked with Apple. Some detailed play records may remain until the 28-day threshold described in section 6 and the next daily cleanup and are then deleted automatically. Minimal records strictly required for legal obligations, payment/refund handling, fraud prevention, or legal claims may remain until those purposes end. Store transaction records managed independently by Apple or Google are not deleted by deleting the game account; restorable purchases can later be restored to a new game account as described in the Terms.

    8. Advertising and Analytics Consent Choices

    • Optional usage analytics: one optional choice jointly controls House Duck first-party usage analytics stored in Supabase (including the stability diagnostics in section 2) and Firebase Analytics (including automatically collected events and tutorial completion). Users aged 18 or over make an explicit choice that is not preselected, separate from required terms acceptance and advertising consent. Declining does not restrict the game. Essential account, currency, transaction, and security processing is separate.
    • The choice can be changed or withdrawn at any time in the app settings (“Optional usage analytics”). Withdrawal stops future collection and transmission for both optional analytics systems, discards the app-managed unsent queues, and resets the Firebase Analytics data on the device. It does not affect processing lawfully completed before withdrawal; deletion of information already transmitted follows sections 6 and 9.
    • Ad-campaign measurement: we promote Quirky Ball with Google Ads app campaigns. Only if you gave the optional usage-analytics consent, the Firebase tutorial-completion event may be used by Google to count how many installs from those campaigns finished the tutorial (conversion measurement). Google may link this to its own advertising data under Google’s policies. Without that consent, no such event is sent.
    • Older app versions: the Firebase-only notice and setting in older Quirky Ball 1.x versions such as 1.1.2 (build 53) control Firebase Analytics only, not first-party analytics stored in Supabase. A previous Firebase-only ON choice does not retroactively extend to the combined scope; a fresh explicit choice on the new notice is required.
    • Ads: Quirky Ball shows Google AdMob rewarded, interstitial, banner, and native ads. A Google UMP message may be shown in regions that require consent, including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is shown separately when cross-app tracking permission is requested.
    • Where required, you can reopen Ad Privacy Settings in the app settings and can also manage advertising identifiers and tracking in device settings. Declining advertising consent does not prevent gameplay, but rewards conditional on completing an ad view may be unavailable.
    • Whether advertising is personalized, limited, or non-personalized does not by itself settle whether consent is required. The requirement depends on applicable law and the particular storage or access to a device. In Germany, TDDDG Section 25 may require consent for applicable storage or access.

    9. Your Rights

For requests to which the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month. Where necessary because a request is complex or numerous, we may extend that period by up to two further months and will tell you within the first month, including the reasons.     You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation, and at any time to processing for direct marketing. Withdrawing analytics consent or resetting the device SDK does not mean that all previously stored server data is immediately erased; contact support separately about historical deletion. Korean users may also seek help through the personal-information complaint service (118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee.

    10. Children's Privacy

    Our games are not directed to children under 14, or a higher minimum age required by local law. In Quirky Ball, you cannot enter the game before confirming your age (unknown age: 0) or if you are under 14. Users aged 14–17 can play, but ad SDK/Google UMP requests and optional usage analytics are disabled for them. Users aged 18 or over can proceed to the standard advertising-consent flow after accepting the required terms; ads are not requested without the required consent. You select your birth year and month on the age-confirmation screen; the age band is calculated only on the device. The day of birth is not requested, and the selected year/month is not saved or transmitted. Because the day is unknown, the age-14 and age-18 thresholds are conservatively treated as met only from the month after the relevant birth month. Only the calculated band is stored locally (0: unknown, 14: ages 14–17, 18: age 18 or over). This does not replace identity/age verification or any required parental authorization. If we learn that information from an underage user was processed, we will verify and delete it.

    The game’s age-14 requirement is separate from the age for data-processing consent. Where local law requires parental or guardian authorization for consent-based processing, that authorization is required. In Germany, online consent-based processing covered by GDPR Article 8 generally requires parental authorization below 16; this is not a blanket prohibition on all gameplay. The on-device age-band calculation is not legal age verification or a parental-consent mechanism. Contact support about children’s data.

    11. Security

    House Duck uses reasonable safeguards including TLS, access controls, Supabase Row Level Security, least privilege, server-side purchase verification with Apple and Google, and duplicate-transaction controls. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

    12. Required Information and Automated Decisions

    A guest UID, basic game state, and the Supabase-backed operations that store and synchronize them are required to provide our games. Essential account, currency, transaction, and security processing is separate from optional analytics. Supabase-backed usage analytics and Firebase Analytics can be declined or withdrawn together; declining does not restrict the game. Without optional sign-in or advertising consent, some features (for example friends, invitations or loading your account on another device) or personalized advertising may be unavailable. Automated fair-play checks may withhold a reward or a ranking entry that fails a plausibility check; you can ask support for a human review. House Duck does not make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.
