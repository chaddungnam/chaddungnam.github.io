      1. Overview

      House Duck ("we", "House Duck") respects the privacy of 10KEY ("the game") players and follows applicable data protection law, including the GDPR. This notice explains what personal data we process, why, where it is stored, who it may be shared with, how long we keep it, and how it can be deleted.

      10KEY is a web game, first published on CrazyGames (later also planned for Android and iOS). It is played without a House Duck account. This notice covers the web version on CrazyGames. Before we release a version that uses other sign-in methods or other data, we will update this notice first.

      • If you are not signed in to CrazyGames, nothing about you is sent to our server. Your progress is stored only on your device (browser storage).
      • If you are signed in to CrazyGames, the game sends the result of a run (one play from start to end) that beats your best of the week to our server for the rankings (details in section 2).

      2. Information We Collect

      10KEY does not create House Duck accounts and does not ask for an email address, password or nickname; it uses your CrazyGames username. Game progress and settings stay on your device (browser storage) and are not sent to our server.

      Separately, like any online service, when the game contacts our server (including the ranking reads above) our hosting provider technically receives standard connection data such as your IP address and the time of the request. We do not use it to identify or track you, and it is not part of the ranking data.

      3. Why We Use It

      • showing the weekly and all-time rankings, and your position ("you are #N") in the game;
      • checking that submitted results are plausible (anti-cheat);
      • limiting how often results can be submitted (rate limits).

      4. Who We Share Information With

      5. Retention

      • Players who are not signed in to CrazyGames: nothing is stored on our server.
      • Player record and ranking entries: until you ask for deletion. A player not seen for 6 months is deleted with everything stored about them.
      • Weekly boards: weekly entries older than 12 weeks are deleted.
      • Run records: ordinary run records are deleted after 35 days; runs flagged by our checks are deleted after 90 days.
      • Progress and settings on your device: until you clear your browser storage.

      6. Your Rights and Deletion

      Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or object to processing, by contacting support@houseduck.in. We normally respond within 30 days.

      • For an access or deletion request, email us and include your CrazyGames username. We delete your ranking data from the 10KEY server: your player record, ranking entries and run records.
      • If you play again while signed in to CrazyGames after a deletion, a new ranking entry is created when a run is submitted.
      • If you are not signed in to CrazyGames, there is nothing on our server to delete; you can clear your browser storage to remove progress stored on your device.
      • For your CrazyGames account and the data CrazyGames holds, contact CrazyGames under its privacy policy.

      7. CrazyGames, Ads and Tracking

      • 10KEY is played on CrazyGames and uses the CrazyGames SDK, for example for sign-in and to report when gameplay starts and stops.
      • The CrazyGames SDK may show ads and may collect data. That is done by CrazyGames under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.
      • We do not personalize ads, we do not sell data, and we do not run any other analytics or advertising tracking in the game.

      8. Children

      The game is not directed at children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. CrazyGames handles age and consent on its platform, and a player who is not signed in to CrazyGames sends us nothing. If we learn that we have collected a child's data, we delete it immediately. A legal guardian may request deletion using the contact below.

      9. Security

      • Row level security is enabled on every database table; the game has no direct access to the tables. Rank lists are read through restricted server functions, and results are accepted only by a server function that first verifies your CrazyGames login token.
      • Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
      • Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and server secrets are never included in the game.
