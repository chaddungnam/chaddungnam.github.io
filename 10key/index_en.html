How it plays

Dig, pick, dodge, repeat

  1. Break keys, dig down

    Hold a direction to keep digging. The deeper you go, the harder the keys get.

  2. A card at every level-up

    Pick one of three cards to add a new skill or power up one you already have.

  3. Dodge the mouse

    It marks a red lane, then charges. Stay out of the red.

  4. Come back stronger

    Spend the scrap you bring home on permanent upgrades like the drill, battery cell and motor.
