10key Survival 딸깍 서바이벌

How it playsShort runs. The deeper you dig, the harder the keys.

  1. Smash keys, dig down

    Hold a direction to keep digging. The deeper you go, the harder the keys.

  2. A card every level-up

    Pick one of three cards: a new skill, or a stronger one you already have.

  3. Dodge the mouse

    The mouse marks a red path, then charges. Get out of the red.

  4. Come back stronger

    Spend the parts you bring back to upgrade the drill, battery and motor for good.
