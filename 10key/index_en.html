HOW TO PLAY

Dig, pick, surviveThe only way out is down. Get as deep as you can before your tenkey breaks.

  1. Dig

    Hold a direction. Keys crack and shatter; scrap, gold keycaps and chests pop out.

  2. Pick a card

    Every level-up: 1 of 3 cards. A new skill, a stronger skill or a repair.

  3. Escape NUM LOCK

    Caged? Type the code shown to break out.

  4. Golden fever

    2x score while it lasts.

SURVIVE & EVOLVE

Survive, then evolveThe deeper you go, the more comes up from under the keys. Your tenkey evolves too.

What hunts you

The mouse

A red lane lights up and the mouse charges. Dodge it; if it grabs you, mash left and right.

The spacebar

The spacebar slams down from above. When the warning shows, watch where it lands and get out.

How your tenkey evolves

  1. START

    Tenkey

    You start as a living numpad.

  2. LV 7

    DRIFT

    The DRIFT card turns it into a sliding, drifting keyboard.

  3. LV 14 · FULL GAME

    ROBOT

    With DRIFT on, TRANSFORM turns it into a robot. Fall once and you drop back to DRIFT, same run.

When a run ends, spend scrap on permanent upgrades, dig again and climb the monthly ranking.

FAQ

Questions

How much does it cost?

The price is shown in your local currency in the store and is adjusted for local living costs. The price in the store is the one that counts, and it can differ with the Steam release price or promotions. It is a paid game with no ads.

How much will it cost?

It is a paid game with no ads. The price is shown in your local currency in the store and is adjusted for local living costs.

Can I play on a computer?

Yes. The demo (up to level 8) runs right in your browser on itch.io, with no install and no sign-up. A Steam version is coming in 2027.

Can I play before launch?

Yes, the demo (up to level 8), as a pre-registration perk. Modules come with the full version. Once you pre-register, the page shows the itch.io demo link. We recommend playing the itch.io demo on a computer (PC). The phone version is coming to Google Play and the App Store. Already pre-registered? Enter the same email again and the link shows up again. No second email is sent.

Where is my browser-version progress saved?

In the browser version you play as a guest, and your progress is saved in that browser only. Clearing site data deletes it, and it does not move to other devices. The ranking tab shows the online board, but your own runs stay on that device.

What do I get for pre-registering?

One email on launch day with the download link. The same link opens the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. No other marketing email.

The receipt email did not arrive.

Your pre-registration is done the moment you press Pre-register. If the receipt from support@houseduck.in is not in your inbox, check your spam folder. You will get the launch email either way.

How do I cancel?

Use the cancel link in the receipt email, or write to support@houseduck.in. Cancelled addresses are deleted within 30 days.
