How to playThe only way out is down. Get as deep as you can before your battery runs dry.

Dig

  • Hold a direction to dig. Keys crack and shatter; scrap, batteries, gold keycaps and chests pop out.
  • Every level-up, pick 1 of 3 cards: a new skill on one of your keys, a stronger skill, a battery refill or a repair.
  • NUM LOCK keys cage you: type the digits shown to break out. Deep down, walled NUM LOCK zones stand in the way.
  • The run ends when the battery is empty or the tenkey breaks. Spend scrap on upgrades and dig again: every run shows you a little more.

Survive

  • From level 7 the mouse never leaves: it rests, then hunts you again. Keep moving.
  • From level 14 the spacebar stays too and slams down in a cross. Watch where it lands.

Evolve

  • From level 7 the DRIFT card can turn your keypad into a drifting keyboard.
  • From level 14 the TRANSFORM card can turn the drifting keyboard into a robot.

Questions

Can I play before launch?

Yes: the free preview runs on the 10key Survival page on itch.io, right in your browser, on a computer or a phone. No download or sign-up.

Where is my preview progress saved?

In the browser version you play as a guest, and your progress is saved in that browser only. Clearing site data deletes it, and it does not move to other devices. The ranking tab shows the online board, but your own runs stay on that device.

What do I get for pre-registering?

One email on launch day with the download link. The same link opens the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. No other marketing email.

The confirmation email did not arrive.

Check your spam folder for mail from support@houseduck.in. After 10 minutes you can sign up again with the same address to get it again.

How do I cancel?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of our email, or write to support@houseduck.in. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 30 days.
