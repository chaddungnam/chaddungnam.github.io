# Google Play badges

Unmodified official SVG artwork downloaded 2026-09-06 from Google Partner Marketing Hub:
https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/?folder=86718

Source files: GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Web_color_{Korean,English,German}.svg and GetItOnGooglePlay_Badge_Print_color_Japanese.svg (the Japanese file is supplied in the official Digital/svg folder).

Render at 54px high, preserve aspect ratio, and reserve at least 14px clear space on every side. Do not recolor, crop, distort, or add effects to the artwork.

## Custom light variants

The `*-light.svg` files are House Duck custom variants explicitly requested by the owner on 2026-09-06: white background, black lettering, unchanged colored prism, geometry, and gray border. They are NOT official Google-issued light badges and are not represented as Google branding-compliant. Original downloads above remain unmodified.

# App Store badges (added 2026-11-18, 10key Survival launch)

`app-store-{ko,en,de,ja,zh}.svg` are the unmodified official black "Download on the App Store" badges, downloaded
2026-11-18 from Apple Marketing Tools (https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/{ko-kr,en-us,de-de,ja-jp,zh-tw}).
Apple: use only the official badge, do not modify it, keep it at least 40px high on screen, keep clear space around it
(at least one-tenth of its height), and show it only where the app is actually available on the App Store.

`google-play-zh.png` is the official Traditional Chinese "Get it on Google Play" badge
(https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/zh-tw_badge_web_generic.png, downloaded 2026-11-18,
unmodified; the PNG includes Google's own clear space). The 10key pages use the official dark Google badges
(`google-play-{ko,en,de,ja}.svg`, not the custom `-light` ones) next to the black App Store badge, both at the same height.
A store that is not live yet shows plain text ("coming soon"), never a badge.
