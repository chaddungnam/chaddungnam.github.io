      1. Overview and Acceptance

      These Terms govern Quirky Ball, provided by Junhee Choi under the name House Duck in Germany.

      By using the game, you agree to these Terms and the shared Privacy Policy.

      2. The Service

      Quirky Ball is an advertising-supported mobile puzzle game for Android and iOS. It includes:

      • Ad-based monetization — rewarded, interstitial, banner, and native ads (Google AdMob)
      • Optional account linking through Google Sign-In on Android or Sign in with Apple on iOS
      • Supabase-backed guest accounts/authentication, game-state synchronization, online rankings/social features, purchase entitlements/reward state, and first-party operational analytics
      • Friends and gifts, mailbox, and profile/marble customization
      • Gameplay using virtual currency such as stamina and gems

      The game offers optional one-time purchases, including Remove Ads, the Starter Package, and the yakwon Package.

      3. Age Requirement

      The Software may only be used by persons aged 14 or older. Where local law requires a higher minimum age, that age applies. If we learn that a user is below the applicable age, we may delete the related account and data.

      4. Accounts

      • You can play as a guest without any sign-up; in that case a guest identifier (UID) is generated on your device and the server.
      • On Android, Google Sign-In can be used for optional account linking and progress synchronization; on iOS, Sign in with Apple provides the corresponding account-linking option.
      • Guest account data cannot be recovered once the app is deleted. You are responsible for managing your account and progress data.

      5. Purchases and Virtual Items

      • Remove Ads: a restorable one-time product that removes ads and permanently unlocks speed play.
      • Starter Package: a one-per-account package containing one profile icon, one profile frame, three nickname changes, and three country/faction changes. Its exact contents are shown in the app or store before purchase.
      • yakwon Package: a one-per-account, restorable profile icon and marble skin entitlement.
      • Starter Package contents are granted once per account; used components are not replenished by restoring a purchase.
      • Payments are processed through Google Play Billing on Android and App Store/StoreKit on iOS. The store displays price, tax, and payment terms.
      • Restorable entitlements require the same store account. Android uses Google Play purchase status; iOS provides an App Store restore-purchases option.
      • Refunds and withdrawal rights follow store rules and mandatory consumer law; statutory rights are not limited.
      • When a refund, cancellation, or chargeback is confirmed, the Operator may revoke entitlements such as ad removal or cosmetics and reasonably adjust remaining tickets or change rights granted by that purchase. The user may contact support about the decision and its basis and request a review.
      • A legitimate refund request, a genuine unauthorized or minor purchase report, or the exercise of mandatory consumer rights is not by itself grounds for a sanction.
      • Stamina and gems are virtual currencies granted free of charge in the game or earned by actions such as watching rewarded ads.
      • Virtual currency has no cash or real-world value and may not be transferred or exchanged outside the store. This does not limit refunds or restoration rights required by mandatory law or store policy.

      6. Advertising and Optional Analytics Consent

      • The Software displays ads served by Google AdMob; watching rewarded ads is optional.
      • Usage analytics through Firebase Analytics is a separate, optional choice and is not preselected. If allowed, automatically collected events and app-use information such as tutorial completion may be analyzed. This control applies from Quirky Ball 1.1.2 (build 53).
      • Declining Firebase Analytics does not restrict the game, its features, or rewards. The analytics choice can be changed or withdrawn at any time in the app settings.
      • The Firebase analytics choice is separate from advertising consent through Google UMP and the iOS ATT choice.
      • Users in regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK) may be shown a Google UMP consent flow. Advertising choices can be managed through the UMP flow and device settings.
      • Data processing related to ads and analytics is governed by the Privacy Policy.

      7. Leaderboards and Social Features

      • Leaderboards publicly show your nickname/display code, best score, and country to other users.
      • Leaderboard data is stored on Supabase servers, and entries are kept for the most recent 28 days only.
      • Inappropriate nicknames or abnormal scores may be removed from leaderboards on verifiable facts and proportionately, with review through support where possible.
      • Social features may show your chosen nickname, country, best score, tier, and profile customization to other users. Friend requests, relationships, and gift status are stored on the server to provide those features.

      Leaderboard removal, nickname measures, or social-feature restrictions are based on verifiable facts and applied proportionately to the violation. Where possible, we give reasons and an opportunity to request review through support. A legitimate refund or rights request is not abuse by itself.
