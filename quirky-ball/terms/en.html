      These Terms govern Quirky Ball, provided by Junhee Choi under the name House Duck in Germany.

      By using the game, you agree to these Terms and the shared Privacy Policy.

      2. The Service

      Quirky Ball is an advertising-supported mobile puzzle game for Android and iOS. It includes:

      • Ad-based monetization — rewarded and interstitial ads (Google AdMob)
      • Optional account linking through Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple
      • Online leaderboards powered by Supabase
      • Friends and gifts, mailbox, and profile/marble customization
      • Gameplay using virtual currency such as stamina and gems

      The game offers optional one-time purchases, including Remove Ads, the Elite Package, and the yakwon Package.

      • Virtual currency has no cash or real-world value and cannot be refunded, transferred, or exchanged.
      • Virtual currency and some progress may be stored and synchronized on the device and in server account state. They are lost when the account is deleted or the service ends and cannot be restored.
