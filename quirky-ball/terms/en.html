      Use of the game requires acceptance of these Terms.       2. The Service

      Quirky Ball is a free-to-play, advertising-supported mobile puzzle game for Android and iOS. It includes:

      • Merge-puzzle runs, the lab with its abilities, upgrades and reagent collection, and related events
      • Ads — rewarded, interstitial, banner, and native ads (Google AdMob)
      • Guest play without sign-up, and optional account linking with Google Sign-In (Android and iOS) or Sign in with Apple (iOS)
      • Supabase-backed accounts, game-state synchronization, rankings, friends and gifts, invitations, mailbox, profile customization, purchase entitlements and reward state
      • Virtual currencies and items such as gems, reagent and tickets
      • An in-app Quick Report for sending problem reports to House Duck

      The game offers optional one-time (non-consumable) purchases: Remove Ads, the Starter Package, the Yakwon Package (yakwon), and VIP 1. There are no recurring payments.

      4. Accounts

      • You can play as a guest without any sign-up; a guest identifier (UID) is then created on your device and the server.
      • You can optionally link your game account to Google (Android and iOS) or Apple (iOS). A linked account can be loaded on another device by signing in with the same Google or Apple account. Some features, such as friends and invitations, require a linked account.
      • If you sign in with a Google or Apple account that is already linked to another game account, the app asks you first; if you continue, that account’s saved progress is loaded and the current guest progress on the device is replaced and cannot be recovered.
      • Guest risk: guest progress is tied to the app installation. If you delete the app, reset the device, log out, or lose the device without linking an account, guest progress and items may be permanently lost. Please link an account to keep your progress. This explanation does not limit purchase-restoration or refund rights under law or store policy, or the Operator’s legal obligations.

If the account already owns Remove Ads when the tickets would be granted, 2,000 gems are sent to the mailbox instead of the tickets, because speed play is already unlocked.
      • Yakwon Package: a permanent, restorable package of yakwon cosmetic items (such as a profile icon and marble skin).

Restoration checks your store purchase history and copies the permanent entitlements to the game account currently signed in. One-time bonuses, such as the Starter Package’s Speed Tickets or the gems replacing them, are granted only once per purchase and are not granted again by restoring, including to another game account.
