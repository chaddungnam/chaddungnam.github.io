      Fastest way — delete directly in the app

      1. Open the Quirky Ball app.
      2. Go to Settings from the home screen.
      3. Tap Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

      Once in-app deletion is confirmed, the account profile, synchronized state, and social connections stored on the server are deleted and cannot be recovered. Some raw play and archived records may remain for up to 28 days after creation and are automatically deleted according to their retention period. Some current play records may be detached from the account connection; this does not mean that all records are deleted immediately. Device-only settings can be removed separately by uninstalling the app or using device controls.

      No access to the app — request by email

      Use the button below to email us. Nicknames and display codes may be visible to other users and are not authentication. Do not send passwords or full payment details, such as full card numbers. We will explain only the minimum additional information needed to process a request, and we do not process a request based on a nickname and display code alone.

      Data that is deleted or retained

      Data typeDeletion / retention policy
      Account profile (guest identifier (UID), nickname, display code, country)Deleted in-app, or within a reasonable period after an email request is verified
      Linked Google account info (email, profile name)Deleted through the same process
      Server account state (progress, virtual currency, best score, etc.)Deleted through the same process
      Raw gameplay, season, weekly, and archived recordsMay remain for up to 28 days after creation and are automatically deleted according to their retention period
      Device-only settings (language, volume, vibration, frame rate, etc.)Removed when the app is uninstalled or the account is reset
      Advertising identifiers and store transaction recordsManaged by Google or Apple under their policies; minimum payment records may be retained where legally required

      Minimal records needed for legal obligations, payment/refund handling, fraud prevention, or legal claims, and transaction records independently managed by Apple or Google, may remain until those purposes end. Other data is deleted.

      Request verification and response

      For erasure or rights requests to which the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month. Where necessary because a request is complex or numerous, we may extend that period by up to two further months and will tell the user within the first month, including the reasons. If a deletion request must be refused or limited, we will explain the applicable exception and available next steps.
