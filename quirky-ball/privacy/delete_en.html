      Fastest way — delete directly in the app

      1. Open the Quirky Ball app.
      2. Go to Settings from the home screen.
      3. Tap Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

      Once confirmed, all game data stored on our servers and your device is permanently deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

      No access to the app — request by email

      Use the button below to email us. Please include your nickname and display code (shown at the bottom of the in-app Settings screen) so we can verify your account. After verification, we will process the request within a reasonable period and reply with the result.

      Data that is deleted or retained

      Data typeDeletion / retention policy
      Guest identifier (UID), nickname, display code, countryDeleted immediately in-app, or within a reasonable period after an email request is verified
      Linked Google account info (email, profile name)Deleted through the same process
      Server account state (progress, virtual currency, best score, etc.)Deleted through the same process
      Raw gameplay, season, and weekly recordsDeleted with an account-deletion request; otherwise automatically deleted 28 days after creation
      Device-only settings (language, volume, vibration, frame rate, etc.)Removed when the app is uninstalled or the account is reset
      Advertising identifier (ADID)Managed by Google under its own privacy policy (Google policy)

      We retain data after deletion only where a longer retention period is required by applicable law; otherwise no data is kept.
