      1. Overview and Operator

      This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Quirky Ball (the "Service"), provided by House Duck, an independent developer based in Germany (the "Operator").

      The Operator takes user privacy seriously and aims to comply with applicable laws, including South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable U.S. state privacy laws. This Policy explains what information the Service collects, how it is used, and how it is stored and deleted. It is the full version of the privacy notice shown inside the app.

      The controller within the meaning of the GDPR is the Operator of the Service, reachable at the email address listed below.

      2. Information We Collect

      You can play as a guest without any sign-up. The Service collects and stores the following information.

      The Service is currently offered on Android as a free, advertising-supported game and does not offer in-app purchases using real money.

      3. Purposes and Legal Bases

      • Saving your progress and syncing it across devices when linked to a Google account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract)
      • Providing leaderboards — showing nickname/display code, best score, and country (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
      • Serving ads (Google AdMob) — personalized ads based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), non-personalized ads based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
      • Analyzing errors and improving service quality (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
      • Complying with applicable laws and resolving disputes (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)

      5. International Transfers

      Because of the cloud infrastructure we use (Supabase, Google), personal information may be stored and processed on servers outside your country — including outside the EU/EEA. Such transfers rely on the safeguards and legal frameworks applied by those providers (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

      6. Retention Period

      • When you request account deletion, personal information is deleted promptly or within a reasonable period.
      • Raw gameplay, season, and weekly records are kept for the most recent 28 days and automatically removed afterward.
      • Your Supabase Auth account and current account state synchronized to the server are kept until you delete your account and are deleted upon an account-deletion request. Local caches are deleted when the app is uninstalled or the account is reset.
      • Settings stored only on the device, such as language, volume, vibration and frame rate, are deleted when you uninstall the app.
      • Data may be retained longer where required by applicable law.

      7. Your Rights and Deletion Procedure

      • You may view your account info, change your nickname or country, or request account deletion (including deletion of personal information) at any time from the Settings screen.
      • In-app account deletion: go to Settings → Delete Account. Deletion permanently removes all game data from the server and your device and cannot be undone. See the Account Deletion page for details.
      • For access, correction, or deletion requests that cannot be handled in the app, contact us at the email address below.
      • EU/EEA residents may exercise GDPR rights to access, rectify, erase, and port their data, and to restrict or object to processing, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority. California residents may exercise their CCPA rights (the Service does not sell personal information).

      8. Advertising and Consent

      • The Service includes ads served by Google AdMob; watching rewarded ads is optional.
      • Users in regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK) may be shown a Google UMP consent flow. Advertising choices can be managed through the UMP flow and device settings. If you decline, non-personalized ads may still be shown.
      • Google AdMob may use advertising identifiers for ad personalization. You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

      9. Children's Privacy

      The Service is not directed at children under the age of 14 (or a higher minimum age where required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child below that age has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or legal guardians may request deletion via the contact email below.

      10. Security

      The Operator takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. All communication with the server is protected by TLS encryption, and access to the Supabase database is restricted through Row Level Security (RLS).
