      Browser storage and guest accounts

      The guest identifier, session token, settings and consent choices are stored in your browser (IndexedDB/local storage). We use this storage only to run the game and keep your guest account; we do not use it for advertising or tracking. If you clear browsing or site data, play in a private window, or use a browser (such as Safari) that deletes website storage after a period without a visit, the identifier may be lost and the guest account may become unrecoverable on that browser. We cannot restore it, and there is no transfer from a web guest account to a Quirky Ball app account. Playing in another browser or on another device creates a different guest account.

      3. Purposes and Legal Bases

      • Providing the game, guest accounts, rankings, mailbox, in-game reports and support: contract performance or pre-contractual steps (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
      • Legally required records and the handling of legal claims: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
      • Security, fair play, stability, and error investigation necessary to operate and protect the service: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
      • House Duck first-party service analytics stored in Supabase (service improvement and bug fixing): legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)). Collected only after you accept the Terms; you can object at any time under Section 9.

      The legitimate interests are protecting accounts, preventing fraudulent scores or duplicate rewards, investigating errors, handling reports efficiently, and maintaining service stability. Random installation/session identifiers do not by themselves make data anonymous. They also include improving the service by checking game balance and tutorial flow and fixing errors. These analytics are processed only on House Duck’s own servers, are never used for advertising, third-party analytics or personal profiling, and the raw events are deleted after 28 days (Section 6).

      6. Retention

      • Detailed play, season and weekly records and account-separated first-party analytics events: normally 28 days from the record-specific time explained below.
      • Account, current synchronized state, best records, progress and fair-play/reward records: until account deletion.
      • In-game reports: while needed to handle the report, and no longer than until account deletion.
      • Mailbox items: deleted 90 days after they are claimed or expire.
      • Support emails: as needed to resolve the request and handle disputes.
      • Data in your browser (guest identifier, session token, settings, consent choices): in your browser until you clear it or the browser removes it. Clearing it does not delete the server account (see section 7).
      • Information independently held by CrazyGames and other providers: under each provider's retention policy and settings.

      How retention is calculated: daily cleanup removes data older than 28 days based on the play time for detailed gameplay, the update/achievement time for season/weekly records, and server receipt time for first-party analytics. The next daily run can normally add up to one day after that threshold. The 28-day window does not apply to CrazyGames’ or other providers’ independent logs.

      7. Account Deletion

      You can delete your guest account in the game under Settings → Delete Account, follow the account deletion instructions, or email support@houseduck.in. The account profile, synchronized state, progress, mailbox, in-game reports and fair-play records linked to the account are deleted and cannot be recovered. Some detailed play records may remain until the 28-day threshold described in section 6 and the next daily cleanup and are then deleted automatically. Minimal records strictly required for legal obligations, fraud prevention, or legal claims may remain until those purposes end.

      Clearing your browser data does not delete the server account; it only removes the identifiers stored in your browser. If you can no longer use the game on that browser, email us. Nicknames and display codes may be visible to other users and are not authentication, so we may ask for the minimum additional information needed to process the request; do not send passwords or payment details.

      8. Advertising and Usage Analytics

      • Service analytics: The web version has no separate optional analytics choice. After you accept the Terms and this policy, the first-party usage analytics in Section 2 are stored, as part of the service, only on House Duck’s own servers (Supabase) and are not passed to any analytics or advertising provider.
      • To object to analytics, contact support@houseduck.in. We will stop further analytics for that guest account and delete analytics already received under Section 6, or earlier on request.
      • Ads: House Duck does not show advertising of its own in the web version. If ads are shown later, they come through the CrazyGames SDK under CrazyGames’ policies, and any consent or ad choices are managed by CrazyGames.

      9. Your Rights

      Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, or withdrawal of consent by contacting support@houseduck.in. For requests to which the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month. Where necessary because a request is complex or numerous, we may extend that period by up to two further months and will tell you within the first month, including the reasons. The right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority is unaffected. The competent authority in Hesse is Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

      You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation, and at any time to processing for direct marketing. Objecting to analytics does not automatically erase previously stored server data at once; you can ask support separately to delete historical data. Korean users may also seek help through the personal-information complaint service (118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee.

      10. Children's Privacy

      Quirky Ball is suitable for all ages. The web version does not ask for your birth date or age and does not calculate or store an age band. We do not ask for real names, contact details or precise location, and nicknames can be chosen freely. We do not run personalised advertising or profiling directed at children.

      Where local law requires a parent’s or legal guardian’s consent for a child to use the service or for the processing of the child’s data, that consent must be obtained before playing. A parent or guardian who wants to access or delete a child’s data can contact support@houseduck.in; after verification we delete the guest account and its data.

      11. Security

      House Duck uses reasonable safeguards including TLS, access controls, Supabase Row Level Security and least privilege. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

      12. Required Information and Automated Decisions

      A guest identifier, basic game state, and the Supabase-backed operations that store and synchronize them are required to provide the web version. Service analytics are processed as part of the service after you accept the Terms; objections follow Sections 8 and 9. Because the web version has no sign-in, some features (for example friends, invitations or loading your account on another device) are unavailable. Automated fair-play checks may withhold a reward or a ranking entry that fails a plausibility check; you can ask support for a human review. House Duck does not make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.
