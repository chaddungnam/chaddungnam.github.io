      2. The Service

      Quirky Ball is a free-to-play puzzle game that you play in your browser. The web version includes:

      • Merge puzzle runs, abilities and upgrades, the lab with reagent collection, and related events
      • Automatic guest play without registration (there is no Google or Apple sign-in on the web)
      • A Supabase-based guest account, game-state storage, rankings, and in-game Quick Report to send problem reports to House Duck

      Features that need a linked account (for example friends and invitations) are not available in the web version.

      Not offered on the web: in-app purchases, advertising by House Duck, rewarded ads, sign-in with Google or Apple, and push notifications. Paid items are available only in the Android and iOS apps.

      4. Accounts

      • You play as a guest without registering. When you first play, a guest identifier (UID) and a session token are created automatically and stored in your browser (IndexedDB/local storage). Your progress is stored on House Duck’s server (Supabase) under that guest account.
      • The web version has no sign-in and no account linking. There is no transfer between a web guest account and a Quirky Ball app account. Playing in another browser or on another device creates a different guest account.
      • Browser storage risk: your guest account is tied to the data stored in this browser. If you clear browsing or site data, play in a private window, or use a browser (such as Safari) that deletes website storage after a period without a visit, the guest identifier may be lost and the account, progress and items may become unrecoverable on that browser. This notice does not limit any statutory rights or the operator’s legal obligations.
