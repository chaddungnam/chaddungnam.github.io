The hardest part of the Google Play closed test was

not making the game

 

but gathering 12 Android users.

 

I thought it would end quickly if I asked family and friends.

 

However, there were more iPhone users than expected,

and even for those with Android phones, asking them to maintain test participation status for 14 days

was not a casual favor to ask.

 

 

12 users were gathered from the 'SmallDevFeedback Hub'.

Relying only on acquaintances had clear limitations.

 

So SmallDevFeedback Hub was used,

and eventually over 12 users could be recruited.

(Not an ad, a Google tester recruitment app exclusively for Koreans)

 

Google's official condition for individual developer accounts is

that at least 12 users participate in closed testing for 14 consecutive days

before applying for production access.

 

Google Play Official Closed Testing Guide

 

That is how the numbers were met.

 

However, what changed the game was not a number like 12

but the persistent feedback from close acquaintances with Android phones.

 

 

Over 14 days, the game turned over a new leaf.

If you put the first build

and the last build side by side,

 

it is almost embarrassing to call them the same game.

 

Therefore, starting from the opening,

growth selection, reward animations, profile, and shop,

I kept fixing them in the exact order players experience them.

 

 

It was not something that was finished by fixing it once.

 

New builds were uploaded on the same day,

feedback was received again,

and it was replaced again a few hours later.

 

 

Vibe coding

made the fixing speed fast,

but what actually needed to be fixed

 

was told by someone with decades of experience,

an experienced person.

 

Even I, who did QA for a long time,

was blinded rather easily in front of my own game.

 

 

In the end, what mattered was not the number of people.

12 users were just the passing condition.

 

However, one or two people who clicked through enthusiastically

and pointed out weird parts without sugarcoating

truly elevated the completeness of the game.

 

Currently, Quirky Ball has finished the 14 day test

and applied for production access.

 

 

It is not approved yet.

 

Still, if these 14 days had not existed,

I likely would have believed a much less polished game was the finished product

and launched it as is.

 

The Google platform is now ready.

 

 

Note:
