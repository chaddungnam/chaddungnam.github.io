The process of uploading an app to Apple was

not something that ended with just preparing the game build.

 

As I started preparing for the iOS release of Quirky Ball,

I found that the address registered in App Store Connect

was still listed as the German address from before my move.

 

I thought I just needed to change a single line of address.

 

However, at Apple, because this address was linked to the contract,

it was not a matter of fixing it directly in the profile.

 

 

The address in App Store Connect was the old address.

First, I logged into the Apple Developer account

and checked the membership details.

 

The program was registered normally, but

only the address remained as the information from before the move.

 

 

I clicked 'Update your information' at the bottom of the screen

and requested an address change from Apple Developer Support.

 

Even in the official Apple guide,

it states that to change the name or address associated with the Developer Program,

you must submit a change request and have it verified with supporting documents.

 

Official guide for changing Apple Developer account information

 

In other words, rather than editing the address input field and saving it,

it was a procedure where Apple checked the new address and then reflected it.

 

 

A request for supporting documents came after 3 to 4 business days.

After sending the address change request,

in my case, a reply came after about 3 to 4 business days.

 

This period is just my actual experience,

and does not mean that all requests are processed within the exact same time.

 

The email asked to upload one of the documents

containing the new address.

 

Examples included a valid government-issued ID,

a postal address change application,

an address verification document from the local residence office,

or recent materials like utility bills, bank statements, and official government correspondence.

 

 

I decided to organize the front and back of my German residence permit showing my current address

into a single file and submit it.

 

Up to this point, it was understandable even if annoying.

 

The problem was the link sent to upload the file.

 

 

The automatic link in the email did not open properly.

When I clicked the link in the email,

instead of the file upload screen,

a screen appeared saying 'This account does not have file upload permissions'.

 

 

Eventually, I re-checked the internet address written in the email.

 

Either a period attached to the end of the sentence in the email got mixed into the address,

or the value after ?teamId= that distinguishes accounts

was not included fully to the end in the automatic link.

 

To put it simply for those without development knowledge,

the long internet address sent by Apple was cut off incorrectly in the middle.

 

In my case, I solved it like this.

I manually copied the email address from beginning to end.

 

Then, excluding the 'period' attached like punctuation at the very end of the address, and

including the identifier value after ?teamId= down to the very last character,

I pasted it into the browser address bar.

 

Only then, with the same Apple account,

was I able to enter the normal file upload screen.

 

In the screenshots of this post as well, names, addresses, and account identifiers were all covered up.

 

 

 

 

Upload success and address change completion are not the same thing.

After uploading the document,

a screen displayed 'Your document is being processed'.

 

What was confirmed here was only that the document had been successfully received.

 

Whether the address change was finally approved and

the new address was reflected in App Store Connect

had to be checked again.

 

In the process of uploading Quirky Ball to the Apple market,

administrative tasks like this keep popping up one by one.

 

Even if you do not know code,

you can prepare an app,

but having very small knowledge like distinguishing where a link ends

can sometimes save you time.

 

For those who, like me, from the Apple Developer address change link

If you suddenly get a message saying you do not have upload permissions,

before continually switching accounts, check if the period at the end of the URL and

the value after ?teamId= are all fully included first.
