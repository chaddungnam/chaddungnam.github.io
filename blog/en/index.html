The reason why I suddenly started developing after coming all the way to Germany

I am from the Department of Industrial Design at Kyonggi University, class of 17. Now, I have established a sole proprietorship in Germany and am trying to create and release various software including games myself. My very first game, Quirky Ball, is practically finished in development, and I have reached the stage of business registration and payment testing for both major markets. But the funny thing is, I originally had no intention of becoming a developer.
