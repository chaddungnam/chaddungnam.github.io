Why I Suddenly Started Building Software After Moving to Germany

I entered Kyonggi University’s industrial design program in 2017. Today I run a sole proprietorship in Germany and am preparing several software products, including games, for release. My first game, Quirky Ball, is nearly finished; I am now working through business registration and payment tests for both app stores. The funny part is that I never planned to become a developer.
